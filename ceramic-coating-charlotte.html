Why coat your paint?

Wax and spray sealants wear off in weeks. A ceramic coating forms a hard, slick layer on top of your clear coat that lasts for years. Dirt has a harder time sticking, water beads up and rolls off, and a normal wash brings the shine right back.

Carolina summers are rough on paint. Strong sun fades and oxidizes it, pollen season coats everything yellow, and highway grime bakes on. A coating adds a sacrificial layer between all of that and your paint, so your car keeps that deep, rich color longer.

Every coating starts with a full exterior wash and a clay bar treatment to pull out bonded contaminants, so the coating bonds to clean paint. If your paint has swirl marks or light scratches, we recommend paint correction first, because a coating locks in whatever is underneath it. Correction is quoted separately.

FAQ

Ceramic coating questions

How long does the ceramic coating last?

Our coating is rated for 3 years. Regular hand washes help it reach that full lifespan.

Do I need paint correction before a ceramic coating?

Not always, but a coating seals in whatever is under it, including swirl marks and light scratches. If your paint has visible defects, correction first gives you the best result. Paint correction is quoted separately.

Why do I need a garage or covered space?

The coating has to go on clean, dry paint out of direct sun and rain. A garage or covered area keeps dust and weather off while it's applied.

How much does a ceramic coating cost?

It depends on your vehicle's size and the condition of the paint. Request a free quote and we'll get back to you the same day.

How should I wash my car after it's coated?

Hand washing is best. Skip automatic car washes with brushes, which can wear the coating down faster. Our Exclusive Member maintenance plan can handle the upkeep for you.

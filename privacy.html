Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

YousefCleanz ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile car detailing business serving Charlotte, North Carolina and the surrounding area. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request a quote using the form on our website, we collect the information you enter:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and city
  • Details about your request: vehicle size, the service you're interested in, and your requested date

We only collect this information when you choose to submit the form. If you call, text, or email us, we receive whatever information you share in that message.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and give you pricing
  • To schedule, confirm, and carry out your detailing appointment
  • To contact you about your appointment or service

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your permission.

Services that help run this website

We use a few trusted third-party services to operate this site. They receive only what they need to do their job:

  • Formspree receives your quote form submission and delivers it to our email.
  • Google Fonts, OpenStreetMap (the service-area map), and unpkg (map software) load parts of the page. Like most websites, these services may see your device's IP address and browser type when the page loads.
  • Links to Google, Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to your request, provide our services, and keep basic business records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you by contacting us using the details below. We'll respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
